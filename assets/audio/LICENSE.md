# Demo audio provenance

`ambient-3m.mp3`, `ambient-5m.mp3`, and `ambient-9m.mp3` are original instrumental samples synthesized specifically for this project from sine waves using `scripts/create_demo_audio.py`. They contain no third-party recordings or voices. These audio samples and the synthesis recipe are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/.

They are static development samples, not final Re-Wired FM recordings. Lesson text is reflective reading material; there is no spoken narration in these files. No in-app audio generation occurs. Start at a comfortable volume.
